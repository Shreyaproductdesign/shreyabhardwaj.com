# Design Context

## Target audience
Recruiters, hiring managers, and design peers reviewing Shreya Bhardwaj’s portfolio — especially product design / design-engineering roles.

## Use cases
- Quickly understand who she is and what she makes
- Browse selected case studies and playground work
- Reach out via LinkedIn / resume / email

## Brand personality / tone
Playful but precise. Dark cinematic hero, warm beige type, pixel sun as a quiet easter egg. Confident product-designer voice with vibe-coder energy — not corporate, not chaotic.

## Constraints
- Vite + React + TypeScript
- Keep Chakra Petch for hero display; General Sans body; Manrope nav
- Prefer CSS motion (transform/opacity/filter); respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Left-aligned compositions; glass Apple-style nav
